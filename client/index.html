    Each bro has a crib with a front and back door. Doors are locked by default. Bill Collectors are blind and hunt by sound: footsteps, sprinting, smashing and knocking all give you away. Walk mode is slow but nearly silent.

    • WASD move, C walk mode (quiet), Shift sprint (loud), Space jump
    • F ring light: mogs a Bill Collector that gets close, burns aura
    • E open your door, or knock on a friend's (they choose to let you in)
    • R hold to repair your doors, or fix the generator in a power failure
    • Y/N answer a knock, Tab scoreboard, Enter chat

    Got collected? You're in NPC mode. A living bro standing next to you for 3 seconds brings you back. Survive until the timer runs out to touch grass.

    BRO CHASE

    Survive the night. Bill Collectors are coming for what you owe.

    • Your crib has a front and a back door. Both stay locked unless you open them.
    • Bill Collectors are blind: they hunt by sound. Footsteps, sprinting and knocking give you away.
    • If one smashes in, run out the other door to a friend's crib and knock. They decide if you get in.
    • Grab cash and spend it in the shop (B): stronger doors, banana peels, energy drinks, decoys, bribes.
    • Got collected? A living bro next to you for 3 seconds brings you back. Survive until the timer hits 0.
